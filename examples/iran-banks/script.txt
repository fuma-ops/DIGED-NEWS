America is fighting Iran with a weapon you can't see: banks.
On Monday, the US hit Iran's secret money network. Twenty-two companies and eight people that quietly move Iran's cash. One of them handles tens of billions of dollars a year.
Here's the idea. Iran sells oil, but it needs a way to bring the money home. These secret banks are that way. Cut them, and the cash gets stuck. And any company still doing business with Iran now risks being punished too.
Iran's answer? The Strait of Hormuz, one of the most important oil routes on Earth. Iran says it stays closed until America ends the war, lifts its naval blockade and drops the sanctions.
That's why it matters to you. When that route is blocked, the world's oil supply is at risk, and that can show up in your gas price.
This war isn't only fought with missiles. It's fought with money. Follow to understand why the world is changing.
